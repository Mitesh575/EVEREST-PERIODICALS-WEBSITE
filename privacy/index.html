Enquiry privacy information

This information relates to the public journal subscription enquiry form.

Information collected

The form requests a contact name, designation, institution, department, phone number, email address, institution address, and journal requirements. Certain fields are optional.

How enquiries are handled

When submitted, form information is transmitted through EmailJS, an external email delivery provider, using the agency's email configuration. The information is used to respond to subscription enquiries. Do not enter passwords, government identification numbers, medical details or payment-card information into free-text fields.

Questions and requests

Contact everestch91@gmail.com for questions, corrections and deletion requests about submitted enquiry details. The agency must still confirm its actual retention periods, additional recipients and relevant privacy procedures before this becomes a complete privacy policy.
